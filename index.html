COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you export

Does MarketPack upload my product photos?

No. Image decoding, resizing, JPG conversion, naming, and ZIP creation run in this browser. The current page has no backend, analytics, cookies, or storage.

What does the free prototype support?

Up to three JPG, PNG, or WebP source images and JPG folders for Etsy, Amazon, Shopify, and Instagram. Choose fit and pad to preserve the whole image or fill and crop when edge cropping is acceptable.

Do the exports guarantee marketplace approval?

No. They are convenient size presets, not compliance certification. Check current category, content, background, file-size, and rights rules before uploading.
