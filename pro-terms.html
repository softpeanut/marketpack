Processing contract

One run accepts 1–20 JPG, PNG, or WebP images and creates selected marketplace JPG folders plus manifest.csv in one ZIP. Source filenames become safe output slugs. It includes fit/pad and cover/crop, a padding color, and JPG quality controls. Files are processed in browser memory and are not uploaded. Large batches may exceed browser or device memory.

Order and delivery

A public order request is not payment and must contain no images or private information. After confirming availability, the seller posts an issue-bound, single-use BOLT11 invoice for 15,000 or 100,000 sats according to the selected tier, with a 30-minute payment window. The returned invoice status endpoint lets the seller verify settlement without public payment proof. Within one business day of verified payment, the issue author's GitHub account is invited to a buyer-only private repository containing the licensed ZIP and SHA-256 checksum.

Support and refunds

Report a reproducible download or execution defect in the buyer-only repository within seven days. If it cannot reasonably be resolved, the paid sats are returned to a compatible Lightning invoice supplied by the buyer. Mandatory consumer-protection rules override conflicting terms. A preferred crop or rejection by an external marketplace is not a guaranteed product defect.
